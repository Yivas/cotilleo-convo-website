# Code of Conduct

Participation should be respectful, specific and focused on the work. Disagreement about content or design does not justify personal attacks. Boundaries, privacy and requests to stop unwanted contact must be respected.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information and repeated disruptive posting are not accepted. Responsibility for submitted content remains with its contributor, including when generated by an agent.

These rules apply to issues, pull requests, any project discussion spaces and public representation of this project. They do not claim authority over unrelated communities. Yivas may remove content, close threads, limit participation or block an account according to severity and repetition, without exposing unnecessary personal details.

Visible abuse can be reported through GitHub's **Report content** control. [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/contact) handles private or safety-sensitive platform reports; there is no separate confidential maintainer inbox at present. [Issues](https://github.com/Yivas/cotilleo-convo-website/issues) can receive non-sensitive moderation requests, not private evidence. The vulnerability channel is not an abuse-report channel. No response time or outcome is guaranteed.
